Secret Santa

Privacy & terms

Secret Santa is a small, free, non-commercial website for organising gift exchanges between friends, families and colleagues: people join a group, share wishlists, and each person is secretly given someone to buy a gift for. This page explains what information the site uses and why. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. We only ask Google for this basic profile information.
  • What you enter: your display name and avatar, the groups you create or join, your wishlist items (and any links you add), chat messages, and whether you have bought your gift.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site stores your login session in your browser so you stay signed in.

How it is used and who can see it

  • To sign you in and run your groups and the name draw.
  • Players in your group see your display name, avatar and wishlist.
  • Your group's admin also sees the name, email and photo of your Google account when you ask to join or are a member, so they can make sure nobody pretends to be someone else.
  • Who you are buying for is shown only to you. In the anonymous chat, your giftee cannot see who you are.
  • The person who runs this website can access the information stored by the service when needed to operate it, fix problems, and prevent abuse or unfair play.
  • We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores the site's data and handles sign-in.
  • Google provides sign-in with your Google account.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.
  • Microlink: when you paste a shop link into your wishlist, the link is sent to Microlink to fetch a preview picture and title. Pictures and shop logos are then loaded from those sites (and Google's icon service).

Use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sign you in and identify you to your group.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information is kept while your groups exist. A group's admin can delete their group, and you can leave a group before its draw. To have your account and data deleted, email secretsanta19557@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Terms of use

  • Use your own Google account and your real name, and be kind in wishlists and chats.
  • Don't try to find out who has whom, spoil the surprise, or misuse the site. Players, messages or groups that break these rules may be blocked or removed.
  • The site is provided free and as-is, without guarantees. Gifts are arranged between players; the site isn't responsible for them.
